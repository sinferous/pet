                          1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Luna is a cross-platform desktop companion application — a small,
animated pixel-art cat that lives directly on your screen.

The purpose of Luna is simple:

  * To bring warmth and personality to an otherwise cold, utilitarian
    desktop environment.

  * To serve as a gentle, non-intrusive wellness tool that reminds
    users to stay hydrated throughout the day.

  * To provide a small moment of delight and emotional connection in
    the middle of long work sessions.

Luna is not a productivity tool. Luna is not a game. Luna is a
companion — something that exists alongside your work, quietly making
your digital space feel a little more alive.

                    2. THE PROBLEM LUNA SOLVES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Modern desktop environments are designed purely for function. They are
efficient, but they are also sterile. People spend 8–12 hours a day
staring at screens — at spreadsheets, code editors, email clients, and
browser tabs. There is no warmth. No personality. No life.

This creates two tangible problems:

  PROBLEM 1: SCREEN FATIGUE & EMOTIONAL DISCONNECT
  -------------------------------------------------
  Long screen time leads to a kind of emotional numbness. Users report
  feeling drained, isolated, and detached after extended work sessions.
  The desktop itself offers zero emotional engagement — it is a tool,
  and nothing more.

  Luna addresses this by introducing a living, breathing presence on
  your screen. The cat walks, sleeps, plays, reacts when clicked,
  follows your cursor, and performs spontaneous animations. It is a
  small thing, but it creates a genuine emotional micro-interaction
  that breaks the monotony of the workday.

  PROBLEM 2: DEHYDRATION DURING LONG WORK SESSIONS
  -------------------------------------------------
  Studies consistently show that people forget to drink water while
  working at a computer. Dehydration leads to reduced concentration,
  headaches, fatigue, and decreased cognitive performance — the exact
  things that harm productivity.

  Most reminder apps solve this with intrusive notifications or
  pop-ups that interrupt workflow. Luna takes a different approach:
  the cat itself runs to the center of your screen and holds up a
  water bottle with a gentle, playful message. The reminder feels
  like it comes from a companion, not from a system alert. This
  makes users far more likely to actually act on it.

               3. WHY FUN IS NECESSARY (NOT OPTIONAL)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

There is a common misconception that fun and productivity are opposing
forces. That anything "fun" on a computer is a distraction. This is
wrong, and here is why:

  * MICRO-BREAKS IMPROVE FOCUS. Research in cognitive psychology shows
    that brief moments of positive emotion — even something as small
    as watching a cat animation for 2 seconds — can reset attention
    and reduce mental fatigue. Luna provides these micro-breaks
    passively, without requiring the user to actively seek them out.

  * EMOTIONAL DESIGN DRIVES ENGAGEMENT. Users who feel an emotional
    connection to their environment are more engaged, more satisfied,
    and less likely to burn out. A pet that reacts when you pet it,
    follows your cursor, or curls up and sleeps when idle creates a
    subtle but real sense of companionship.

  * PLAYFUL REMINDERS ARE MORE EFFECTIVE. A cold notification that
    says "Drink water" is easy to dismiss. A small cat that sprints
    across your screen and holds up a tiny bottle is charming — and
    charming things get attention.

  * PERSONALITY MAKES SOFTWARE MEMORABLE. The most beloved software
    products are the ones with personality. Luna turns a desktop —
    something purely functional — into something with character.

Fun is not a luxury. Fun is a design decision that makes software
more humane, more engaging, and ultimately more effective.

              5. COMPLETE FUNCTION & FEATURE BREAKDOWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━


  5.1  PET BEHAVIOR & ANIMATION SYSTEM
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Luna has 14 distinct behavioral states, each with its own animation
  set and timing rules. The behavior is governed by a finite state
  machine called the BehaviorMachine.

  State            Frames   Duration       Description
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  IDLE             4        5–15 sec       Standing still, gentle bob,
                                           occasional blink
  WALK             4        10–25 sec      Walking to a random target
                                           across screens
  RUN              4        5–15 sec       Fast sprint with forward lean
                                           and streaming tail
  SLEEP            2        10–20 sec      Curled-up pose with floating
                                           "Zzz" overlay
  DRINK            4        6 sec          Picks up a water bottle and
                                           tilts it to drink
  PLAY             3        5–9 sec        Squash/stretch bounce with
                                           happy eyes
  REACT            2        2.5 sec        Startled/shy response when
                                           clicked or petted
  FOLLOW           Walk     10 sec         Tracks cursor position in
                                           real time
  LAUGH            3        3–5 sec        Happy-eyes, wide mouth,
                                           animated teardrops
  JUMP             4        3–5 sec        Squash-and-stretch vertical
                                           hop with Y offset
  ROLL             4        4–7 sec        Full 360° tumble rotation
  WOOLBALL          —       5–8 sec        Bouncing yarn ball physics
                                           simulation
  CHEER             —       3–5 sec        Confetti particle burst that
                                           trickles down
  LOVE             3        4–6 sec        Heart-shaped eyes, blush
                                           cheeks, floating hearts

  The BehaviorMachine uses weighted randomness to pick the next
  activity after each idle period. The probability distribution
  ensures variety — the cat won't repeat the same action too often.

  If the cat is idle for 30–60 seconds with no user interaction,
  it automatically falls asleep.


  5.2  MULTI-MONITOR NAVIGATION
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Luna is aware of all connected displays and can walk seamlessly
  between them.

  Key functions:
    * ScreenRect         — Represents a single display's bounds
    * ScreenNavigator    — Handles pathfinding across displays
    * pickTarget()       — Selects a random point on any display
    * step()             — Advances position toward target by
                           speed, handling diagonal movement
    * adjacentScreen()   — Detects neighboring displays within
                           a 4px tolerance for seamless crossing

  Edge behavior:
    * At a display boundary with an adjacent screen → cross over
    * At a display boundary with no neighbor → stop and turn around
    * Vertical edges → clamp to stay fully on-screen


  5.3  WATER HYDRATION REMINDER SYSTEM
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  The hydration system is Luna's core wellness feature.

  How it works:
    1. A configurable timer fires at the user's chosen interval
       (1, 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes).
    2. Luna sprints to the CENTER of the primary display at 2.5×
       normal walk speed ("Hydration Sprint").
    3. The DRINK animation plays — Luna picks up a water bottle.
    4. A speech bubble appears with one of 10 randomized messages
       (e.g., "💧 Stay hydrated, human!", "💧 Hydrate or dry-rate!").
    5. The bubble persists with a red "×" close button until the
       user manually dismisses it.
    6. When dismissed, Luna immediately sprints away to a random
       location ("Dismiss Sprint").

  Key design decisions:
    * No random meows or purrs — reminders are ONLY water-focused.
    * The alert does NOT auto-dismiss — this forces acknowledgment.
    * The sprint-to-center guarantees the user sees the reminder
      regardless of where Luna was on screen.

  Related functions:
    * triggerWaterHydrationFlow()   — Begins the sprint sequence
    * triggerWaterReminderBubble()  — Shows the speech bubble
    * closeSpeechBubble()          — Dismisses and triggers run
    * WaterReminderManager (macOS) — Manages timer and settings


  5.4  CLICK-THROUGH TRANSPARENCY
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Luna's window is fully transparent and borderless. The key
  technical challenge: the user must be able to click THROUGH
  the transparent parts of the window to interact with apps
  underneath, while still being able to click ON the cat itself.

  Solution:
    * Per-pixel alpha hit testing on every mouse move
    * If the pixel under the cursor has alpha < 8 → ignore mouse
      (pass through to apps below)
    * If the pixel has alpha >= 8 → the cat is under the cursor,
      capture the event
    * On Windows (Electron): uses setIgnoreMouseEvents(true/false)
    * On macOS: uses NSWindow's ignoresMouseEvents with alpha
      sampling from the SpriteKit scene


  5.5  DRAG & DROP INTERACTION
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Users can pick up Luna and drag her anywhere on screen.

  Implementation:
    * mousedown on the cat → records offset between cursor and
      cat position, enters REACT state
    * mousemove while dragging → updates position in real time
    * mouseup → drops the cat at current position (no snap-back)
    * Dragged position is clamped to the active monitor bounds
    * On Windows: uses Electron IPC for global cursor tracking
    * On macOS: handled natively via SpriteKit mouse events


  5.6  SPEECH BUBBLE SYSTEM
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Luna can "speak" through pixel-art-styled speech bubbles that
  appear above her head.

  Bubble types:
    * Water Reminders  — Persistent, with red close button
    * Cat Voices       — Auto-dismiss after 3 seconds
    * React Voices     — Shown when petted, 3 seconds
    * Custom "Say"     — Triggered from menu, 4 seconds

  Water reminder messages (10 total):
    "💧 Stay hydrated, human!"
    "💧 Glug glug! Water time!"
    "💧 Drink water now!"
    "💧 Keep your batteries full!"
    "💧 Hydrate or dry-rate!"
    "💧 Be like a plant: water!"
    "💧 System alert: Drink water!"
    "💧 Take a sip of water!"
    "💧 Hydration check!"
    "💧 Drink up!"


  5.7  PROCEDURAL SPRITE GENERATION
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  All of Luna's animation frames are generated procedurally from a
  single base SVG file (Cat.svg) using the generate_frames.js script.

  The system:
    * Parses the base Cat.svg into body paths, tail backing, and
      tail highlight layers
    * Applies per-frame transforms: translate, rotate, scale
    * Generates overlays for expressions:
        - Closed eyes (sleep blink arcs)
        - Happy eyes (upward arcs)
        - Heart eyes (SVG heart paths for LOVE state)
        - Blush cheeks (pink circles)
        - Laughing mouth (wide/medium open mouth)
        - Teardrops (small/medium/large for laugh animation)
        - Water bottle (positioned/rotated per drink frame)
    * Outputs 38+ SVG frame files across 13 animation directories
    * Frames are written to BOTH Windows and macOS asset folders

  This means Luna requires NO heavy image files — all art is
  vector-based and procedurally composed.


  5.8  CELEBRATION EFFECTS (macOS)
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  The macOS version includes SpriteKit particle effects:

    * CONFETTI (Cheer state) — Colorful rectangular confetti pieces
      burst upward, then fall with gravity, bounce off walls, and
      fade out. Uses 7 vibrant colors. A gentle trickle continues
      while the cheer state is active.

    * HEARTS (Love state) — ❤️ emojis drift upward with a sine-wave
      wobble, clamped to window bounds, fading over 1–2 seconds.

    * WOOL BALL (Woolball state) — An orange ball bounces around the
      window with simple physics (gravity, wall collisions, damping).
      The cat faces toward the ball while playing with it.


  5.9  SYSTEM TRAY / MENU BAR
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Both platforms provide system-level controls:

  macOS (Menu Bar 🐱 icon):
    * Toggle Prevent Sleep (on/off)
    * Toggle Water Reminders (on/off)
    * Set Hydration Interval
    * Set Custom Reminder (set a one-time reminder time and note for today)
    * Toggle Start at Login
    * Toggle Hide/Seek (completely hides the pet window for screen sharing)
    * Idle (Park) / Poke
    * Say
    * Movements submenu (all 13 states)
    * Quit Luna

  Windows & Linux (System Tray icon & Right-click context menu):
    * Same options as macOS (including Hide/Seek, Idle/Poke, and Custom Reminder)
    * Opens a styled Control Panel window for settings


  5.10  SLEEP PREVENTION
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Luna can optionally prevent the computer from sleeping.

    * macOS: Uses NSProcessInfo activity assertion with
      idleSystemSleepDisabled
    * Windows: Uses Electron's powerSaveBlocker API with
      "prevent-display-sleep" mode


  5.11  AUTO-START AT LOGIN
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Luna can be configured to launch automatically at system startup.

    * macOS: Uses SMLoginItemSetEnabled / Launch Services
    * Windows: Uses Electron's setLoginItemSettings API to register
      the app's exe path in the Windows Registry

  Safety checks:
    * Both platforms detect if the app is running from a temporary
      directory (ZIP extraction, mounted DMG) and warn the user that
      auto-start will break after a restart.
    * Windows dev mode: does NOT register startup entries to avoid
      polluting the Registry with development paths.


  5.12  SETTINGS PERSISTENCE
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  User preferences are saved to disk and restored on launch.

    * macOS: Uses UserDefaults via SettingsStore
    * Windows: JSON file at %APPDATA%/Luna/settings.json

  Persisted settings:
    - sleepPrevention (bool)
    - waterReminders (bool)
    - waterIntervalMinutes (int)
    - autoStart (bool)
    - isParked (bool)
    - isHidden (bool)


  5.13  PARKING / IDLE MODE
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  If Luna gets in your way, you can "park" her:

    * Select "Idle (Park)" from the menu.
    * Luna walks to the bottom-left corner of the primary display and sits down.
    * No walking, running, following, or sleeping while parked.
    * She remains parked until manually "Poked" or overridden by a water/custom reminder (which walks her back to the park position automatically upon dismissal).
    * Select "Poke" to wake her up and resume normal wandering behavior.


  5.14  CUSTOM SPRITE SUPPORT
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Users can replace Luna's default art with their own custom sprites:

    * macOS: Place frames at ~/Library/Application Support/
      DesktopPet/Sprites/ in subdirectories
    * Windows: Replace frames in the local artwork/ folder

  Supported animation directories:
    idle/, walk/, sleep/, drink/, play/, react/, follow/,
    laugh/, jump/, run/, roll/, woolball/, cheer/, love/

  5.15  CUSTOM REMINDER SYSTEM (TODAY ALARMS)
  ────────────────────────────────────────

  Users can set custom, one-time reminders with a custom time (e.g. 14:30) and message for today.

  How it works:
    1. The user selects "Set Custom Reminder..." from the menu bar or tray dropdown.
    2. They enter a target time (24h format, e.g., 14:30) and a message note.
    3. An internal clock monitoring loop polls the system time. When the current hour and minute match the target, it triggers the custom reminder sequence.
    4. The pet performs a Hydration Sprint to the center of the primary monitor, sits, and shows the custom message inside a persistent bubble.
    5. The reminder stays locked in place until the "×" close button is clicked, after which it clears itself and returns the cat to normal movement or its parked position.

               7. PRIVACY & PERFORMANCE PHILOSOPHY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  * FULLY OFFLINE — Luna makes ZERO external network requests. No
    telemetry. No analytics. No tracking. No cloud sync. Everything
    runs locally on your machine.

  * ULTRA-LIGHTWEIGHT — Extremely low CPU and memory footprint on
    both platforms. The SVG-based sprite system means no large image
    assets are bundled or loaded into memory.

  * NO SOUND — Luna is intentionally silent. No audio files, no
    system sounds. All communication is visual (speech bubbles and
    animations). This makes it suitable for office environments
    and shared spaces.

                       8. IN SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Luna is a small application with a big heart.

It solves the problem of sterile desktop environments and forgotten
hydration by providing a living, interactive companion that is
charming enough to notice and gentle enough to never get in the way.

Every function — from the weighted random behavior engine to the
per-pixel transparency hit testing to the procedural SVG sprite
system — is designed to make one tiny cat feel alive on your screen.

And that is the entire point.
